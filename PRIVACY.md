# Privacy policy — Eport Journal

*Applies to the Journal apps in this repository (iPhone/iPad, Mac, Windows, Android), including "Eport Journal" on the App Store. Last updated 2026-09-27.*

**The apps collect no data.** There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing you write or read in the app is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

What the app does with your data:

- **Your journal folder.** You choose a folder (or open the built-in sample journal). The app reads the Markdown files in it and, when you tick an action, highlight or remove an item, edit an entry or add a capture, writes to those files. The files stay where they are. If the folder is in iCloud Drive or another sync service, that service syncs them under its own privacy policy; the app does not talk to it directly.
- **The folder reference.** The app remembers which folder you chose, on your device only, so it can open it next time. "Forget folder" in Settings removes it.
- **Links.** Tapping a link in an entry opens it in your browser. The app does not follow links by itself.

The companion Claude Code plugin that writes the journal runs on your own computer and is covered by the README ("Privacy").

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/pylenius/daily-journal/issues>.
